Career Pathways After 12th for Arts Students

The Arts stream is a gateway to careers that shape culture, policy, design, and human understanding. Students can explore diverse fields ranging from law and management to journalism, psychology, and creative design.

Core Academic Courses

BA (3 years) with specializations in Philosophy, Economics, Geography, English, Political Science, History, Psychology, Foreign Languages, or Social Science.

Careers: Civil services, teaching, writing, research, public administration.

Law and Management

  • Law (LLB) – Advocacy, corporate law, public service
  • BBA / BBM – Management principles, marketing, entrepreneurship

Hospitality, Tourism, and Service Careers

  • Hospitality Management – Hotel operations, event planning
  • Hotel Management – Culinary arts, housekeeping
  • Tourism Studies – Travel, geography, business management
  • Air Hostess Training – Aviation hospitality and safety

Creative and Design Careers

  • Fine Arts – Music, dance, painting, sculpture
  • B.Des – Fashion, textile, interior, accessory, leather, knitwear design
  • Journalism – Reporting, writing, media ethics
  • Film Making – Cinematography, direction, editing
  • Advertising – Marketing communication, brand strategy
  • Animation – Digital graphics and storytelling
  • Photography – Visual arts and technical mastery
  • Media Studies – Communication, PR, digital content

Architecture and Design Integration

B.Arch (5 years) for students with Mathematics — blending creativity with technical precision for careers in architecture, urban planning, and interior design.

Education and Teaching

  • Nursery Teacher Training – Early childhood learning
  • TTC – Primary education
  • Bachelor’s Degree – Foundation for teaching
  • B.Ed – High school teaching

Emerging Fields for Arts Students

  • Psychology and Counseling – Mental health, HR, social work
  • Foreign Languages – Translation, diplomacy, international business
  • Social Science Research – Policy analysis, NGO work, development studies

The Arts stream is not a fallback option — it’s a foundation for creativity, leadership, and human understanding. From law and management to design and teaching, Arts students can build careers that influence society and culture.
