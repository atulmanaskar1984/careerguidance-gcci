Frequently asked questions

When should I start career counselling?

The best time is early in your academic journey — during school or college. However, career guidance is valuable at any stage. Whether you're in Class 10, Class 12, college, or a working professional, it's never too late to make smart career choices.

How long does the psychometric test take?

Our psychometric test typically takes 40–60 minutes. It's best completed in one sitting, but your progress is saved automatically in case of interruptions. The counselling session also lasts 40–60 minutes and can be recorded for future reference.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit/debit cards, net banking, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), and e-wallets. We also offer flexible options to suit your convenience.

Do I need to prepare anything before the session?

No preparation needed. Just ensure a stable internet connection and a device with a camera. We encourage parents to join the session with their child for the best results.

Can I get a session outside of India?

Absolutely! Our video counselling sessions are just as effective as in-person meetings. Students and professionals from across the globe can benefit from our services.

What if I'm not satisfied with the service?

Your satisfaction matters to us. If you're not satisfied, we will provide another counsellor to meet your expectations. Please reach out to us at hello@goldencareer.example with your concerns.
